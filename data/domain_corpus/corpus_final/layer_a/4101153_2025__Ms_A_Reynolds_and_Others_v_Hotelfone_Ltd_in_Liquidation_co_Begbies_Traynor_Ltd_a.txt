JUDGMENT OF THE EMPLOYMENT TRIBUNAL

10 The Judgment of the Employment Tribunal is as follows.

1. The complaint that the first respondent failed to comply with its duty to consult under sections 188 and 188A of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 is well founded.

2. A protective award for 90 days’ pay is made effective from 13 March 2025 15 pursuant to section 189 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992.

3. The first respondent is ordered to pay the appropriate remuneration to each affected employee in accordance with section 190 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992.

REASONS

5 1. The first respondent was a limited company providing hospitality reservation services. The claimants were employed by the first respondent until their dismissal on 13 March 2025 due to redundancy. On the same date, Kenneth Robert Craig of Begbies Traynor was appointed joint liquidator. The second respondent is the Secretary of State for Business and Trade. 10 2. The claimants commenced early conciliation on 14 May 2025, with a certificate issued on 19 May 2025. They submitted claims to the Tribunal on 11 June 2025, seeking a declaration under sections 188 and 188A of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 and a protective award under section 189 of the Act. 15 3. The first respondent did not submit a response. However, the claim form included a letter from Mr Craig stating that, due to commercial sensitivity and efforts to preserve the business, consultation with employees was not possible. He raised no objection to any protective award.

4. The second respondent sent a response but indicated they would not be 20 represented at the hearing.

5. The first claimant attended the hearing and gave evidence on her own behalf. Although her evidence was not challenged in cross-examination I found her evidence to be credible and reliable. I made the following findings in fact.

Findings in fact

25 6. The first respondent is a limited company that provided hospitality services from its registered office at 2 Gateside, Prieston Road, Bridge of Weir, PA11 3AJ. The first respondent’s director and centralised finance function operated from this location, where 41 employees were based. During the COVID-19

4101153/2025 and others page 3

pandemic, the first respondent adopted remote working, later transitioning to a hybrid model.

7. There was no recognised trade union in the workplace, nor were there any existing representative bodies with appropriate authority to consult on behalf 5 of employees.

8. The first claimant was aware that the first respondent had faced financial difficulties since the COVID-19 pandemic. The first respondent did not engage in any meaningful consultation with the claimants regarding potential redundancies or the election of employee representatives. 10 9. On 13 March 2025, all employees, including the claimants, were asked to attend a meeting with the joint liquidators either in person at the respondent’s premises or virtually. At that meeting, they were informed that liquidators had been appointed and the business would cease trading that day.

10. On 13 March 2025, all employees of the first respondent were made 15 redundant. The first respondent failed to invite employees to elect representatives for consultation purposes. There were no special circumstances rendering such elections not reasonably practicable.

Deliberations

11. Based on the evidence, I am satisfied that the first respondent dismissed 20 more than 20 employees at one establishment within a period of 90 days or less.

12. There was no recognised trade union or representative body in the workplace. The first respondent failed to ensure the election of employee representatives in accordance with section 188A of the Trade Union and 25 Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, and did not consult as required under section 188.

13. I make a protective award in respect of the redundancies, as the first respondent failed to consult with elected employee representatives. The claimants are therefore entitled to bring this claim.

14. I have decided to make a protective award covering all employees dismissed as redundant at the first respondent’s only establishment at 2 Gateside, Prieston Road, Bridge of Weir, PA11 3AJ. The protective period begins on 13 March 2025 and is set at 90 days. 5 15. The first respondent is ordered to pay the appropriate remuneration for this period.
