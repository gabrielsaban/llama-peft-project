JUDGMENT

1. The claims for breach of contract, unlawful deduction from wages and discrimination arising from disability (under section 15 of the Equality Act 2010) are dismissed upon withdrawal by the Claimant.

2. The claim for breach of the General Data Protection Regulations is dismissed for want of jurisdiction.

3. The Claimant succeeds in her claim that the Respondent failed to comply with a duty to make reasonable adjustments under sections 20, 21 and 39(5) of the Equality Act 2010.

4. The Claimant succeeds in her claim of unfair dismissal in relation to the termination of her employment with the Respondent on the 20th May 2025.

5. The claim of harassment under section 26 of the Equality Act 2010 is dismissed.

6. In respect of the Respondent’s failure to comply with a duty to make reasonable adjustments, the Claimant is awarded the sum of £12,100.00 for injury to feelings.

7. In respect of the Claimant’s unfair dismissal, the Claimant is awarded a Compensatory Award in the sum of £1,689.83 comprising the following sums:

(a) £1,384.35 (gross) for loss of earnings for the period from the 20th May 2025 to the 31st August 2025; and
(b) £305.48 for loss of statutory employment rights. CMD-Ord 2 of 4 November 2023

8. The Claimant is awarded interest at the rate of 4% on the injury to feelings award and the Compensatory Award for the period from the 24th May 2025 to the 11th December 2025 in the sum of £303.75.

9. The total sum awarded to the Claimant is £14,093.58, which is calculated as follows:

(a) £12,100.00 for injury to feelings;
(b) £1,384.35 (gross) for loss of earnings for the period from the 20th May 2025 to the 31st August 2025;
(c) £305.48 for loss of statutory employment rights; and
(d) £303.75 for interest. Employment Judge David Harris Dated: 11th December 2025 Judgment entered in Register and copies sent to parties on 19 December 2025 Katie Dickson for Secretary of the Tribunals CMD-Ord 3 of 4 November 2023
