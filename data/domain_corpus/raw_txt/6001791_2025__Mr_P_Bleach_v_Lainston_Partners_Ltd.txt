JUDGMENT
1. The complaint of unauthorised deduction from wages is well founded. The
respondent made unauthorised deductions from the claimant’s wages in the
period 1st to 17th May 2024 and 10th to 6 November 2024.
2. The complaint in respect of holiday pay is also well-founded. The respondent
made an unauthorised deduction from the claimant’s wages by failing to pay the
claimant for holidays accrued but not taken on the date the claimant’s
employment ended.
3. The respondent shall pay to the claimant the sum of £14,471.91 (which is
£15,371.91 less £900 for which the claimant accepts he should give credit).
This is the gross sum. The claimant is responsible for the payment of any tax
or National Insurance.
