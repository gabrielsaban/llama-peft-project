JUDGMENT
1. The Claimant’s complaint of breach of contract in relation to notice pay is
well-founded.
2. The Respondent’s counter complaint of breach of contract is well-founded.
3. The parties agreed the compensation for damages as set out below.
4. The Claimant is entitled to a payment equivalent to 10 weeks’ net pay -
£11,069.84. The parties agree that damages to the Respondent would be
at £3,500.00. The parties have agreed to offset the amount of £3,500.00
from the sum payable to the Claimant.
5. This judgment records that agreement. Accordingly, the Respondent shall
pay the Claimant £7,569.84.
