JUDGMENT
The unanimous judgment of the Employment Tribunal is as follows:
1. The claim of direct race discrimination is not well founded, and fails.
2. The claim of harassment due to race is not well founded, and fails.
3. The claim of victimisation is not well founded, and fails.
4. The claims of direct age discrimination and sexual harassment are
withdrawn by the Claimant.
5. The respondent has requested written reasons for the judgment, which will
be issued shortly.
