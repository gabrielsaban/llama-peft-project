JUDGMENT
1. The complaint of unfair dismissal is well-founded. The claimant was unfairly
dismissed.
2. The following complaint of unfavourable treatment because of something
arising in consequence of disability (section 15 Equality Act 2010) is well-
founded and succeeds: The claimant was not consulted about remaining
employed, when the decision to keep the respondent’s paint line open was
made.
3. The remaining complaint of unfavourable treatment because of something
arising in consequence of disability is not well-founded and is dismissed.
