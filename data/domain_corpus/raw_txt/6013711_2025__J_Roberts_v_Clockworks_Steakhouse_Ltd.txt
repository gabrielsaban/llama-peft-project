Case Number: 6013711/2025
EMPLOYMENT TRIBUNALS
Claimant: J Roberts
Respondent: Clockworks Steakhouse Ltd
JUDGMENT
1. The claim was presented in the Manchester Employment Tribunal on 18th April
2025. The respondent has failed to present a valid response on time. The
Employment Judge has decided that a determination can properly be made of the
claim, or part of it, in accordance with rule 22 of the Rules of Procedure.
2. The complaint of unauthorised deductions from wages is well-founded. The
respondent made an unauthorised deduction from the claimant’s wages.
3. The respondent shall pay the claimant £2,926.00 (209 hours x £14 per hour) which
is the gross sum. The claimant is responsible for the payment of any tax or National
Insurance.
Approved by:
Employment Judge Slater
2nd October 2025
