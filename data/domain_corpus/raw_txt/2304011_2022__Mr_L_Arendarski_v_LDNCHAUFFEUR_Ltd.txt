JUDGMENT
1. The hearing continues in the absence of the respondent following refusal of the
respondent’s application to postpone the hearing.
2. The claimant was a worker of the respondent.
3. The claimant was not paid for accrued but not taken holiday pay and the respondent
must pay him the sum of £2,712.
4. The respondent made an unauthorised deduction from the claimant’s wages in
March 2022. It was not reasonably practicable for the claimant to bring a claim about
that until he did and his claim was brought in a reasonable time thereafter. The
respondent must pay him the sum of £476.
