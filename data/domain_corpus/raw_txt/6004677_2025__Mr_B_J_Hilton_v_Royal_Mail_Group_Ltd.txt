Case Number: 6004677/2025
EMPLOYMENT TRIBUNALS
Claimant: Mr B J Hilton
Respondent: Royal Mail Group Ltd
JUDGMENT
The claim is struck out.
REASONS
1. By a letter dated 8 August 2025 the Tribunal gave the claimant an
opportunity to make representations or to request a hearing at which to do
so, as to why the claim should not be struck out because
• the claimant had not complied with the Order of the Tribunal dated 17
June 2025; and/or
• it has not been actively pursued.
2. The claimant has failed to make representations in writing, or has failed to
make any sufficient representations, why this should not be done or to
request a hearing. The claim is therefore struck out.
3. The hearing fixed for 5 and 6 February 2026 will not take place.
Employment Judge Phil Allen
6 October 2025
