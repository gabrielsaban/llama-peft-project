Case No: 6020280/2025
EMPLOYMENT TRIBUNALS
Claimant: Harry Hicks
Respondent: Stonehaven Search Limited
JUDGMENT UNDER
RULE 22
1. The Respondent has failed to file an ET3 within the deadline and has not requested an
extension of time to file the same.
2. Having considered the ET1 and attachment provided by the Claimant, Employment
Judge Keogh has decided that a determination of the claim can properly be made
without a hearing and the Judgment of the Tribunal, made under rule 22 of the
Employment Tribunals Procedure Rules 2024, is as set out below.
3. The Respondent has unlawfully failed to pay in full the Claimant’s bonus of £40,000
which was agreed in March 2024. The sum of £4,000.00 net is outstanding.
4. The Respondent unlawfully failed to pay the Claimant’s pension payments in the sum
of £2500 net.
5. Accordingly, the Respondent is ordered to pay the Claimant £6,500 net and to account
to HMRC for any tax and NI due on this sum.
