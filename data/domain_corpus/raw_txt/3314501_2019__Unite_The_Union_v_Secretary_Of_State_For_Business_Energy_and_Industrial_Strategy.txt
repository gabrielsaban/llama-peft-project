Case No:3314501/2019
EMPLOYMENT TRIBUNALS
Claimant: Unite The Union
Respondent: Secretary Of State For Business, Energy And
Industrial Strategy & Others
JUDGMENT
The claim is struck out.
REASONS
1. By a letter dated 01 December 2019 the Tribunal gave the claimant an
opportunity to make representations or to request a hearing, as to why the claim
should not be struck out because
 the claimant had not complied with the Order of the Tribunal dated 8
November 2018, 8 June and 20 October 2019
 it has not been actively pursued
2. The claimant has failed to make representations in writing, or has failed to
make any sufficient representations, why this should not be done or to request a
hearing. The claim is therefore struck out.
