Case Numbers 2305189/2025 & 2306703/2025
EMPLOYMENT TRIBUNALS
Claimant: Daniel Sale
Respondent: Science Card Limited
JUDGMENT
Rule 38(1)(a) Employment Tribunal Rules of Procedure 2024
1. Claim 2305189/2025 is struck out as an abuse of process on the basis that it is a
duplicate of claim 2306704/2025.
REASONS
1. On 19 July 2025 Mr Sale submitted a claim against the respondent for unpaid
wages. That claim has been given case number 2306703/2025.
2. The same claim form named Ms Leigh Golding as a second claimant. That second
claim was given case number 2306704/2025.
3. Ms Golding also submitted a separate claim form, on 9 June 2025. That claim has
been given case number 2305188/2025.
4. She also included Mr Sale on that claim form. That second claim has been given
this case number - 2305189/2025.
5. The result is that four claims have been brought in total.
6. By letter dated 31 July 2025 I asked the claimant to confirm whether he was pursing
both claims and if so why.
7. No reply has been received and so the second claim appears to be an unintended
duplicate. It is dismissed as an abuse of process.
