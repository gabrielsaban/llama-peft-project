JUDGMENT
1. The claimant’s complaint under section 168 Trade Union and Labour
Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 that the respondent failed to permit the
claimant to take time off during his working hours for the purpose of carrying
out trade union duties is well founded and succeeds.
2. We make a declaration that the respondent failed to permit the claimant to
take time off during his working hours for the purpose of carrying out his trade
union duties on 15, 16, 17, 18 and 19 July 2024 and 12, 13, 14, 15 and 16
August 2024.
3. The complaint under section 169 Trade Union and Labour Relations
(Consolidation) Act 1992 is dismissed
4. The respondent made an unauthorised deduction from the claimant’s wages
in respect of pay for the weeks commencing 14 July 2024 and 11 August
2024.
5. The respondent shall pay the claimant the sum of £3027.50 subject to any
deductions for tax, national insurance or pension contributions.
