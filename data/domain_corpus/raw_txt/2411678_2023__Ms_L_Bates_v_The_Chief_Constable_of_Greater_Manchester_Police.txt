JUDGMENT
The unanimous judgment of the Tribunal on remedy for failure to make reasonable
adjustments for disability (Equality Act 2010 sections 20 &21) is as follows:
1. The Tribunal has made no recommendation or recommendations.
2. The claimant is awarded the cumulative sum of £15,000 (fifteen thousand
pounds) for the failure of the respondent to make a reasonable adjustment to
its practice of: (i) allocating the claimant and other case workers a case load of
between 50 and 68 cases; (ii) allocating a caseload of which approximately
90% were complex psychological cases; and (iii) not checking in with the
claimant regularly about her mental wellbeing on from 1st November 2022.
5. We make no award for any financial losses.
6. The claimant is entitled to interest in accordance with the Employment
Tribunal’s (interest on awards in discrimination cases) Regulations 1996:
Interest on the award is £3,470.24, therefore the total award is £18,470.24.
