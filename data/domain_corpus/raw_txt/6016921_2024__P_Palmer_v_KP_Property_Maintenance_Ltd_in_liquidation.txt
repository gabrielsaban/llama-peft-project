JUDGMENT
It is the judgment of the tribunal that:
1. Contrary to Section 13 of the Employment Rights Act 1996 the respondent
has made unlawful deductions from the wages of the claimant in the sum of
£1,069.90 which sum the respondent is ordered to pay to the claimant.
2. The Respondent did not provide the Claimant with a written statement of
initial employment particulars in accordance with s1 of the Employment
Rights Act 1996. The tribunal awards the Claimant two weeks’ pay (£700 per
week gross) a total of £1,400.00.
