REPRESENTATION:
Claimant: In person
Respondent: No attendance
JUDGMENT
The judgment of the Tribunal is as follows:
1. The complaint of breach of contract in relation to notice pay is well-founded.
2. The respondent shall pay the claimant 12 weeks’ notice pay totalling £8,076.96
as damages for breach of contract. This figure has been calculated using gross
pay to reflect the likelihood that the claimant will have to pay tax on it as Post
Employment Notice Pay.
3. The complaint in respect of holiday pay is well-founded. The respondent made
an unauthorised deduction from the claimant's wages by failing to pay the
claimant for holidays accrued but not taken on the date the claimant’s
employment ended.
4. The respondent shall pay the claimant accrued holiday pay of £2,692.40. The
claimant is responsible for paying any tax or National Insurance.
5. Under section 163 Employment Rights Act 1996 it is determined that the claimant
is entitled to a redundancy payment of £16,153.92.
