JUDGMENT
1. From 28 January 2024 (being the pay date for the period December 2023)
to termination of employment on 9 August 2024, there were no
unauthorised deductions of wages and the claimant’s holiday pay was
correctly paid. This claim is therefore dismissed.
2. All claims for unauthorised deduction of wages and unpaid holiday pay prior
to the time period in paragraph 1 are out of time, and it was reasonably
practicable for the claimant to have brought these claims in time. Therefore,
the tribunal does not have jurisdiction to hear these claims.
