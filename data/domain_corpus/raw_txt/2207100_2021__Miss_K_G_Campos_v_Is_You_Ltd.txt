JUDGMENT
BY CONSENT
1. The Respondent accepts that it made unauthorised deductions from the Claimant’s
wages in respect of overtime and holiday pay, in the total net sum of £3,700.
2. The Respondent shall pay the Claimant the amount of £3,700, within the next 28
days. As this represents a net payment, the Respondent shall also ensure that it
has accounted to HMRC for any additional payment due in respect of tax and
national insurance.
3. The Claimant withdraws the unauthorised deductions complaint.
