JUDGMENT
It is the unanimous decision of this Tribunal that
1. The complaint of direct disability discrimination pursuant to section 13 of the
Equality Act 2010 is not well-founded and is dismissed.
2. The complaint of harassment related to disability pursuant to section 26 of the
Equality Act 2010 is not well-founded and is dismissed.
3. The complaint of victimisation pursuant to section 27 of the Equality Act 2010
is not well-founded and is dismissed.
This decision was explained orally to the parties at the hearing.
