JUDGMENT
1. The respondent’s name is amended to GI Media Group Ltd.
2. The claimant’s claim of unauthorised deduction from wages is well-founded and
succeeds.
3. The respondent shall pay the claimant the sum of £450.00 (gross) by 4:00pm on
Monday 20 October 2025.
4. The respondent failed to provide the claimant with a written statement of terms
and conditions of employment contrary to section 1 of the Employment Rights
Act 1996 but there are exceptional circumstances that mean no award under
section 38 of the Employment Act 20002 should be made for the omission.
