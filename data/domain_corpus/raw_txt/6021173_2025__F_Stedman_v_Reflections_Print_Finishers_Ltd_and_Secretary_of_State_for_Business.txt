JUDGMENT
The judgment of the Tribunal is as follows:
1. The complaint under section 189 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations
(Consolidation) Act 1992 is well-founded.
2. The Tribunal orders the Respondent by way of a protective award under
section 189(3) of the 1992 Act to pay the Claimant, a payment equivalent to
remuneration for the period of 90 days commencing 26 February 2025.
3. The Claimant’s gross weekly pay is £679.89. (which has been calculated in
accordance with ss 220 to 229 Employment Rights Act 1996).
4. Thus the protective award is £679.89 x 12.86 weeks = £8,743.39.
5. The Employment Protection (Recoupment of Jobseeker’s Allowance and
Income Support) Regulations 1996 apply to this award.
